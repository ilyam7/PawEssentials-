What This Project Is About

This site explores how climate change is causing sea levels to rise and how that rise is fueling a growing list of coastal hazards — floods, storms, erosion, and the slow loss of habitable land. Sea level rise is not a distant problem. It is happening now, on every coastline on Earth, and it intersects with oceanography, atmospheric science, and human history in ways that affect almost a billion people.

The goal of this project is simple: to make the science clear, to put faces to the consequences, and to show why the choices we make in the coming decades will determine which coastlines survive and which do not.
