Hi. Welcome.

The Ocean's Edge, Explained.

If you've ever stood on a beach and wondered why is the sand here? — or watched the news during a hurricane and thought is this getting worse? — this site is for you. No jargon, no doom-spiral, just a clear look at how our coasts work and why they're changing.

Start with the beach Or jump to rising seas

What you'll find here

We're going to take a short walk along the coast together. We'll look at how beaches actually build themselves, meet the plants and animals that call the shore home, learn why the sea is rising, hear about the storms that change lives, and finish with what people are doing — right now — to give our coastlines a future.

I'm writing this as a college project, but I'm writing it the way I'd explain it to my family at dinner. So pull up a chair.
